# Dividing rigid rows: source reading (dossier §18)

Read for dossier §18, the research proposal on dividing an unknown row
by membership in other unknown rows (`route`, §17). Three notes, each by
an independent reader (research subagents, 2026-10-20), read in full
before they were added here. Each marks what its sources state and what
is the reader's inference. Local copies of the papers were kept outside
the repository and are not stored here.

- boolean-elimination.md: Boole's and Löwenheim's elimination, Boolean
  unification (Martin and Nipkow, JSC 1989), quantifier elimination for
  Boolean algebras and where cardinalities enter.
- boolean-unification-types.md: Boolean unification in implemented type
  systems (Flix: OOPSLA 2020, OOPSLA 2021, ECOOP 2023; the Flix
  compiler source), how rigid variables are treated, and what is
  missing.
- typed-row-theories.md: Rose (POPL 2019), Rω (ICFP 2023), Ur (PLDI
  2010), Leijen (2005): whether typed row theories can divide a rigid
  row.

Not read, and named as leads: Rudeanu's and Brown's books, Büttner and
Simonis (1987), Kozen (1980), Baader (1998), Flix's OOPSLA 2023 and
2025 and POPL 2026 papers (abstract only), Harper and Pierce (1991),
Pottier (ESOP 2000), Rémy ("Typing record concatenation for free").
